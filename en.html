Your home in the south of Tenerife

A spacious, bright, fully renovated two-bedroom apartment in the Granada Park complex, one of the most sought-after areas in the south of Tenerife. It stands out for its layout, space and comfort: ideal for a relaxing holiday.

It has two spacious bedrooms, one with a built-in wardrobe, a full bathroom, a fully equipped kitchen and a bright living room that opens onto a pleasant private terrace with direct access to the pool, perfect for relaxing and enjoying the island's climate all year round.

Its location gives easy access to restaurants, shops and some of the best beaches in the south of the island.
